Digital Marketing Strategy Case Study

Floor Town LLC — B2B Office Carpet Lead Generation (Dubai)

Developing a focused, lean Meta Ads acquisition framework for high-ticket commercial office carpet installations across Dubai's primary business districts.

Project Overview

Floor Town LLC is a flooring and carpet supplier based in Dubai (floortown.ae). The campaign focused specifically on commercial office carpet tiles targeting corporate decision makers.

Objective

Generate qualified commercial B2B quote inquiries via direct WhatsApp messaging and Meta Instant Forms within a lean monthly budget of AED 3,000.

Challenge

Dubai B2B CPMs typically range AED 25–45. Running fragmented funnels on a conservative daily budget would starve the learning phase. The account needed a consolidated structure.

My Role

Digital Marketing Strategist: responsible for campaign architecture, audience segmentation, creative angle definitions, budget allocation, and the 30-day testing plan.

Process

Structured a 2-ad-set CBO framework: Broad B2B prospecting (age 28–55 in Dubai business zones) combined with high-intent warm retargeting of website visitors and WhatsApp openers.

Tools Used

Meta Ads Manager, Meta Pixel & Conversions API, WhatsApp Business API, Canva Pro for ad creative formats, and Google Sheets for weekly CPL tracking.

Final Output

A complete 9-part execution blueprint featuring 30-day scaling rules, creative hook matrices (Before/After, 48h turnaround), and lead qualification scripts.

Key Learning

For high-ticket B2B local offers, Click-to-WhatsApp ads consistently deliver faster qualification and higher trust than complex multi-step landing pages.
